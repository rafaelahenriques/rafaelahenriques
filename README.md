## Featured projects

### [Wolf & Sheep](https://github.com/rafaelahenriques/Wolf-and-Sheep-Game-main) · [Play it ↗](https://wolfandsheepgame.netlify.app)

A turn-based strategy game that I rebuilt on my own from a university Pygame project into a full-stack app. A pure-Python game engine is shared by the desktop game, a FastAPI backend and the test suite. The computer opponent uses minimax with alpha-beta pruning, the client is React and TypeScript, and game history and statistics are stored in PostgreSQL.

`Python` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `SQLAlchemy` `Alembic` `React` `TypeScript` `pytest`

### [Mongmo](https://github.com/rafaelahenriques/mongmo) · [Case study ↗](https://rafaelahenriques.com/projects/mongmo/)

A cross-platform app for a Vietnamese café brand that runs pop-up events across Belgium. Customers get geofenced alerts near live events, check in with a QR code plus GPS, complete camera and motion-sensor challenges, earn rewards and order ahead. Owners get their own interface for managing events, challenges and incoming orders.

`React Native` `Expo` `TypeScript` `Supabase` `PostgreSQL` `TanStack Query` `Zustand`

### [Marina Yee: One Corner Off](https://github.com/rafaelahenriques/momu-marina-yee) · [Visit ↗](https://momu-marina-yee.netlify.app)

An interactive longread about fashion designer Marina Yee, made for MoMu, the Fashion Museum Antwerp. I handled the research, visual design, interaction design and front-end development, with a focus on keeping it responsive, accessible and fast.

`JavaScript` `Vite` `GSAP` `CSS`

### [Dodge Runner](https://github.com/rafaelahenriques/dodge-runner)

A one-to-one WebRTC game that turns your phone into a motion controller. You scan a QR code and tilt the phone to steer a fruit past flies on the desktop screen.

`WebRTC` `Node.js` `Express` `Socket.IO` `Device orientation`

**Currently building:** a trading backtesting engine in Python that replays historical market data through a strategy and reports return, drawdown and Sharpe ratio.
